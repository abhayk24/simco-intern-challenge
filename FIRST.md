# FIRST

**First concern.** Each household appears six times, so 1,200 build visits hold less independent evidence than they seem. Raw gaps look large (environment 24.5% vs low_odor 17.2% entrant share; online 23.7% vs retail 18.5%) but may be noise. Price shows a clear drop (29% at $3.49, 13% at $5.49), but one-visit sales are not repeat buyers.

**First test.** A household-level bootstrap of entrant share by price, message and channel, build set only. Then fit a simple learned choice model and compare its log loss with the starter.

**What would change my mind.** If a message or channel interval excludes zero, I treat that lever as real. If not, I say "I do not know yet" for it.
